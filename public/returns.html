Returns & Refunds

Our hassle-free return policy

Return Window

We accept returns within 7 days of delivery for most products. Items must be unused, in original packaging, and with all tags intact.

Non-Returnable Items

  • Personalized or custom-made products
  • Opened paint sets, inks, or adhesives
  • Items marked "Final Sale" or clearance
  • Gift cards

How to Initiate a Return

  1. Log into your account and go to "My Orders"
  2. Select the order and click "Request Return"
  3. Choose your reason and upload photos (if damaged)
  4. Our team will review within 24 hours
  5. Once approved, schedule a free pickup or drop at nearest center

Refund Process

Payment Method Refund Time
UPI / Wallet 1-2 business days
Debit / Credit Card 5-7 business days
Net Banking 5-10 business days

Damaged or Wrong Item?

If you received a damaged or incorrect item, contact us immediately with photos. We'll arrange a free replacement or full refund — no return shipping needed.
