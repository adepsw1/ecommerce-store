Delivery Coverage

We deliver across India via our logistics partners (Delhivery, BlueDart, DTDC). Most pincodes are serviceable. Enter your pincode at checkout to verify availability.

Order Tracking

Once your order is shipped, you'll receive a tracking link via email and SMS. You can also track orders from your account dashboard under "My Orders".

Packaging

All items are carefully packaged with eco-friendly materials. Fragile items like ceramics and glass art pieces receive extra bubble wrap and rigid box protection.

Delivery Issues?

If your package is delayed, damaged, or lost, contact us within 48 hours at support@yourbrand.in or call +91 98765 43210. We'll resolve it promptly.
