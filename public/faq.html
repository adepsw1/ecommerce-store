Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about orders, shipping, and our products.

Orders & Payment

How do I place an order?
Simply browse our shop, add items to your cart, and proceed to checkout. You'll need to sign in or create an account, then verify your phone number (one-time setup) and enter your shipping details. We accept UPI, cards, and net banking.
What payment methods do you accept?
We accept UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm), all major credit/debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, RuPay), net banking, and Cash on Delivery for select pin codes.
Can I cancel or modify my order?
Yes, you can cancel your order within 2 hours of placing it from your Orders page. After that, if the order hasn't shipped yet, contact us at hello@yourbrand.com and we'll try to accommodate changes.

Shipping

How long does shipping take?
Standard shipping takes 4-7 business days across India. Metro cities (Mumbai, Delhi, Bangalore, Pune, Chennai, Hyderabad) usually receive orders within 3-5 days. Remote areas may take 7-10 days.
Is shipping free?
Yes! We offer free shipping on all orders above ₹999. For orders below ₹999, a flat shipping fee of ₹99 applies.
Do you ship internationally?
Currently, we only ship within India. International shipping is coming soon! Sign up for our newsletter to be notified when we launch international delivery.

Returns & Refunds

What is your return policy?
We accept returns within 7 days of delivery for unused items in original packaging. Handmade and custom-order items are non-returnable unless damaged. Please contact us with photos for damaged items.
How do I get a refund?
Once we receive and inspect the returned item, refunds are processed within 5-7 business days to your original payment method. You'll receive an email confirmation when the refund is initiated.

Products

Are your products artist-grade?
Yes! We curate only premium, artist-grade supplies. Our paints have high pigment concentration, brushes use natural/synthetic bristles, and all products are tested for quality before listing.
Are handmade items truly handcrafted?
Absolutely! All items in our Handmade Crafts and Home Decor categories are made by skilled Indian artisans. Each piece is unique with slight natural variations that make them special.
Can I request a custom/bulk order?
Yes! We offer custom orders for bulk purchases, corporate gifting, and personalized art supplies. Contact us at hello@yourbrand.com with your requirements and we'll provide a quote within 48 hours.
